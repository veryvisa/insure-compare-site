一句话

加拿大省医保管治疗费用；一个人确诊重病后，最先吃紧的通常是收入：自己停工、配偶请假陪护、房贷照还、自费药和康复要花钱。Transition 25 种疾病版做的事很简单：确诊合同列明的 25 种重疾之一、过了等待期，iA 一次性给一笔免税的钱，怎么花由您自己定（iA 产品页）。它是 iA 重疾产品线里病种最全、选项最多的一款，适合当家庭的主力重疾保单。

适合谁、不适合谁

适合：

  • 家里的主要收入人，房贷、孩子学费还要靠这份收入撑十几二十年——重疾给付能顶住治疗期的收入缺口，不用卖房或动退休储蓄。
  • 自雇、小生意主：没有团体福利、没有病假工资，停工一天就少一天收入。
  • 想一次配齐「年轻时便宜的定期＋老了还在的终身」的人：同一产品里 10/20/25 年、到 75 岁、终身都有，还能和 4 种病版本按不同期限、不同保额组合（客户指南）。
  • 担心「早期癌症确诊了却拿不到钱」的人：Prevention+ 对原位乳腺癌、早期甲状腺癌等 7 种情况先赔一部分，而且不减主保额。
  • 给孩子做长期规划的父母：孩子出生即可投保，18 岁前另含 5 种儿童病（见 Transition 儿童投保）。

不适合：

  • 只想用最低预算覆盖最常见的几种大病的人——4 种疾病版覆盖了行业约 85% 的重疾理赔，价格更低（见 Transition 4 种疾病版）。
  • 健康状况复杂、预计全核保会被加费或拒保、又只想要癌症保障的人：可以先看免体检的 CancerGuard。
  • 想用它报销医药费的人：重疾险不是医疗报销险，赔的是确诊后的一次性给付。
  • 预算只够一样保障、而最大风险其实是长期不能工作的人：重疾险赔一次就结束，长期失能要靠失能收入险，两者不能互相替代。

优点、短板与常被忽略的坑

优点

  • 早期病症赔付不吃主保额。 很多早期癌症达不到「危及生命的癌症」定义。Prevention+ 先给 15%，之后若进展成重疾，主保额照赔全额。
  • 期限选项齐。 同一产品覆盖从 10 年期到终身，终身还有 10 年、20 年缴清，可以按「房贷还剩几年、孩子几岁独立」来配。
  • 定期续保不看健康。 期满按原保额续到 75 岁不用重新体检；65 岁前还能转长期。身体变差以后，这一条比价格更值钱。
  • 附带免费第二诊疗意见与就医导航（Novus Health，覆盖本人与家人；页面写明也含心理健康诊断）。

短板

  • 赔一次就结束。 主病种给付后保单终止；癌症复发或再得第二种重疾，这份保单不会再赔。
  • Prevention+ 的上限会「封顶」。 15% 的比例在保额超过约 33.3 万后就被单次 5 万的上限卡住：保 50 万，早期病症也只拿 5 万（10%）。高保额客户别按 15% 算预期。
  • 只有全核保一条路。 视年龄与保额可能要体检、验血；健康状况差的人可能加费、除外或拒保。

常被忽略的坑

  1. 观察期按「开始检查」算，不只按确诊日算。 生效后 90 天内出现症状、开始做检查的癌症，即使 7 个月后才确诊，也不在保障内（客户指南第 13 页有算例）。所以「有点不舒服，先买了再去看」恰恰最容易被拒赔。
  2. 6 个月内必须告知。 癌症、帕金森类、良性脑瘤和 Prevention+ 里的 6 种癌症，确诊后要在 6 个月内把病历交给 iA，否则可能被拒（客户指南第 14 页）。
  3. 境外确诊有额外条件。 诊断要由加拿大或美国执业的专科医生作出；在其他国家确诊的「有特定条件」。常回国的华人家庭，投保前就该问清楚国内确诊要补什么材料。
  4. 续保不是原价。 定期续保免验健康，但新一期费率按续保时的年龄算，会明显上涨。打算长期持有，开始就选长期限，或在 65 岁前转换。
  5. 「保 25 种」不等于「多保了 21 倍」。 按客户指南引用的行业数据，癌症约占重疾理赔 69%、心梗 10%、中风 4%、搭桥 2%，其余合计 15%（Munich Re 2022 调查）。多出来的 21 种病是补「少见但致命」的尾部，价值在于家里有特定风险（比如家族神经系统疾病）时不留空白。

情景推演

以下是假设情景的确定性算例，用来看清钱在什么情况下怎么流动；不是报价，不预示任何人会不会得病。设：35 岁投保，保额 10 万，月保费记作 P（实际金额以 iA 正式报价为准，可用 iA 重疾需求计算器 先估保额）。

情景推演
路径 选 T20（20 年定期） 选 T100（终身，缴到 100 岁）
A. 一直没生病 55 岁期满。累计交了 240 个月 × P。可免验健康续保到 75 岁（新费率按 55 岁算），或在 65 岁前转终身 保障一直在。没有返还附约的话，交出去的保费不退
B. 45 岁确诊早期甲状腺癌（乳头状 T1） Prevention+ 赔 15,000（15%），保单继续，主保额仍是 10 万，还剩 3 次早期病症额度 同左
C. 50 岁心梗，符合定义并活过 30 天 一次性赔 10 万，保单终止 同左
D. 路径 B 之后 52 岁癌症进展到危及生命 主保额 10 万照赔（早期给付不扣减），保单终止 同左
E. 60 岁时才第一次确诊重疾 若 55 岁时没有续保或转换，已无保障；若续保了则照赔 照赔 10 万

三档推演（同一个人在三种健康走向下的结果）：

  • 保守（最坏的财务结果是「没用上」）： 一辈子没得重疾。T20 的总成本最低；T100 不加返还附约则全部保费是「买安心」的成本。想把这部分拿回来，要加保费返还附约，代价是更高的保费。
  • 中性： 中年遇到一次早期病症（路径 B），拿到 15% 应急，主保障不受影响。这是 Prevention+ 最常发挥作用的场景。
  • 乐观（指保障发挥最大作用）： 收入最高、负担最重的年份确诊重疾（路径 C），10 万一次到手，覆盖约一年的收入缺口和自费支出。保额该多大，取决于您要顶住多少个月的收入缺口——这才是配重疾的第一道算术题。

常见问题

赔下来的钱要交税吗？ iA 公开页写明一次性给付免税。公司为员工买、或公司持有保单的情形税务处理不同，要单独问会计师。

确诊了先赔一部分，以后还能赔全额吗？ Prevention+ 的早期赔付不减主保额；以后若确诊 25 种之一并符合定义，照赔全额。

投保后不久查出来癌症，能赔吗？ 生效 90 天内确诊或开始检查的癌症不赔，这是观察期规定。

能不能和 4 种病版本一起买？ 可以。iA 客户指南建议按不同期限和保额搭配两种版本，例如 4 种病定期顶房贷、25 种病终身做底。

我人在国内确诊怎么办？ 合同要求诊断由加拿大或美国执业的专科医生作出，境外诊断另有条件；常回国的家庭投保前要问清材料要求。

iA Financial Group · Critical illness insurance

iA Financial Group Transition – 25 Illnesses

iA's flagship critical illness policy — a lump sum for 25 illnesses, 15% early for 7 early-stage conditions, and terms from 10 years to life.

In-depth guide · checked 2026-11-19 · 6 public sources · scenarios are hypothetical arithmetic, not quotes or promises.

In one sentence

When someone in a family falls seriously ill, the first thing to break is usually not the medical bill — provincial health care pays for treatment — but income: time off work, a spouse taking leave to care, the mortgage, uncovered drugs and rehab. Transition 25 does one simple thing: if you are diagnosed with one of the 25 illnesses defined in the policy and get through the waiting periods, iA pays a tax-free lump sum that you can spend however you choose (iA product page). It is the broadest and most flexible policy in iA’s critical illness line, and fits as a family’s main critical illness coverage.

Who it fits — and who it does not

Fits:

  • The main earner whose income still carries a mortgage and children for 10–20 more years.
  • Self-employed people and small business owners with no group benefits or sick pay.
  • People who want “cheap term while young plus coverage that lasts” in one product: 10/20/25-year terms, to age 75 and for life, and it can be paired with the 4-illness version at different terms and amounts (client guide).
  • People worried about “an early cancer that pays nothing”: Prevention+ pays part of the benefit for 7 early-stage conditions without reducing the face amount.
  • Parents planning long term: children can be insured from birth, with 5 extra childhood illnesses (see Transition for children).

Does not fit:

  • People who only want the most common big illnesses at the lowest cost — the 4-illness version covers about 85% of industry critical illness claims (Transition – 4 Illnesses).
  • People whose health makes full underwriting unlikely and who only want cancer cover — look at no-exam Cancer Guard first.
  • Anyone expecting reimbursement of medical bills — this pays a lump sum on diagnosis.
  • People whose biggest risk is a long inability to work — critical illness pays once; long disability needs disability income insurance.

Strengths, weak spots and easily missed traps

Strengths

  • Early-stage payments do not eat the face amount. If an early condition later progresses to a covered critical illness, the full face amount is still paid.
  • A full range of terms, including permanent coverage paid up in 10 or 20 years.
  • Term renewal without health evidence to age 75, and conversion up to 65 — worth more than price once health changes.
  • Free medical second opinion and health navigation (Novus Health) for the insured and family, including mental health diagnoses.

Weak spots

  • One full claim ends the policy. A relapse or a second critical illness is not paid again by this policy.
  • The Prevention+ cap bites at higher amounts. 15% hits the $50,000 cap above roughly $333,000 of coverage: on $500,000, an early-stage payment is $50,000 (10%).
  • Full underwriting only. Exams and blood tests may be required; impaired health can mean extra premium, exclusions or a decline.

Easily missed traps

  1. The moratorium counts from the first investigation, not only the diagnosis date. A cancer whose symptoms or tests began within 90 days of the effective date is excluded even if diagnosed months later.
  2. Six-month reporting duty. For cancer, Parkinson’s-type disorders, benign brain tumour and the six Prevention+ cancers, medical information must reach iA within six months of diagnosis or the claim may be denied.
  3. Diagnoses abroad carry conditions. The diagnosis must come from a specialist licensed in Canada or the United States; diagnoses made elsewhere are subject to conditions. Families who spend time in China should ask what records are needed before buying.
  4. Renewal is not at the original price. No health questions, but the new term is priced at the age reached.
  5. 25 illnesses is not “21 times more cover”. Per the industry data quoted in the client guide, cancer is about 69% of critical illness claims, heart attack 10%, stroke 4%, coronary surgery 2%, all others 15% (Munich Re 2022). The extra illnesses close the gap on rare but serious risks.

Frequently asked questions

Is the payment taxable? iA’s public page says the lump sum is non-taxable. Corporate-owned or employer-paid arrangements are different; ask an accountant.

If I receive an early-stage payment, can I still get the full amount later? Yes — Prevention+ does not reduce the face amount.

What if cancer is found soon after I buy? Cancer diagnosed or investigated within 90 days of the effective date is excluded.

Can I combine it with the 4-illness version? Yes; iA’s guide suggests pairing versions with different terms and amounts.

What if I am diagnosed outside Canada? The policy requires a specialist licensed in Canada or the U.S.; other diagnoses are subject to conditions.
