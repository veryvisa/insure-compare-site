iA 金融集团（原工业联盟） 儿童重疾附约（Child Critical Illness / Transition Child）

挂在父母保单上的一张附约，覆盖家里现有和将来出生的所有孩子，每个孩子最高 2 万

官方名称：Child Critical Illness rider and Transition Child rider

深度解读 · 核对日期 2026-11-19 · 公开出处 3 条 · 情景推演为假设算例，不是报价，也不是对未来的承诺

一句话

多数家庭给孩子买重疾，最在意的是「万一孩子确诊，父母能有一笔钱先停下工作」，大额倒在其次。iA 的两张儿童附约做的就是这件事：挂在父母的个人保单上（Child Critical Illness 挂寿险等个人保单，Transition Child 挂 Transition 重疾保单），一份附约覆盖家里现有和将来的所有孩子，每个孩子单独最高 2 万（Transition 客户指南第 8 页）。

适合谁、不适合谁

适合：

  • 有两个以上孩子、或打算再要孩子的家庭：新生的孩子自动纳入，不用每个孩子各买一份。
  • 父母已经在买寿险或重疾险，想顺手给孩子加一层基础保障的人。
  • 预算有限、优先保父母、孩子先有一层基础保障的家庭。

不适合：

  • 想给孩子较高保额、或想让保障跟孩子一辈子的人：附约每人只到 2 万，而且挂在父母保单上，要单独投保 Transition（孩子作被保人）。
  • 父母自己的保单打算几年内退掉的人：附约随主保单走。

优点、短板与常被忽略的坑

优点

  • 一张附约管全家孩子，将来出生的也自动覆盖，不用一个个投保、一个个核保。
  • 成本低。 附约按一份计，不按孩子人数各算一份主险（具体以报价为准）。

短板

  • 保额小。 2 万够应付一段时间的停工与交通住宿，不够当长期保障。
  • 依附于父母的保单。 父母保单失效、退保，附约一起没了；它不是孩子自己的资产。

常被忽略的坑

  1. 它和单独给孩子买的保单不是一回事。 附约解决「孩子小时候出事」；孩子长大后的重疾保障，要靠单独保单或孩子成年后自己买。
  2. 儿童病种同样有 25 岁截止。

情景推演

假设情景、确定性算例，不是报价。设：父母一方有一份挂了儿童附约的 iA 保单，家里两个孩子，后来又生了第三个。

情景推演
路径 结果
A. 三个孩子都健康长大 附约一直在主保单上，没有给付；第三个孩子出生后自动纳入，无需另投保
B. 老二 4 岁确诊先天性心脏病（符合定义） 老二这一份赔 2 万；老大、老三的保障不受影响

  • 保守： 用不上，成本是附约那一部分保费。
  • 中性 / 乐观： 某个孩子童年确诊，2 万到手，正好覆盖父母一方请几个月假。路径 C 提醒：附约跟着父母的保单走。

iA Financial Group · Rider

iA Financial Group Child Critical Illness rider and Transition Child rider

One rider on a parent's policy covers every current and future child in the family, up to $20,000 per child.

In-depth guide · checked 2026-11-19 · 3 public sources · scenarios are hypothetical arithmetic, not quotes or promises.

In one sentence

Most families buying critical illness cover for children do not need a large amount — they need “money so a parent can stop working if a child is diagnosed”. iA’s two child riders do exactly that: attached to a parent’s individual policy (Child Critical Illness on an individual policy such as life insurance, Transition Child on a Transition critical illness policy), one rider covers all current and future children under the same policy, up to $20,000 per child (client guide, p. 8).

Who it fits — and who it does not

Fits: families with two or more children or planning more — newborns are included automatically; parents already buying life or critical illness cover; budget-conscious families protecting the parents first.

Does not fit: parents wanting a higher amount or lifelong cover for a child — buy Transition with the child as insured; parents who expect to cancel their own policy soon — the rider goes with it.

How it works

How it works
Item Detail
Attached to Child Critical Illness: an iA individual insurance policy; Transition Child: a Transition policy
Who is covered All current and future children under the same policy
Per child Up to $20,000
Illnesses Childhood illnesses (type 1 diabetes, muscular dystrophy, cystic fibrosis, congenital heart disease, cerebral palsy, before age 25) and Down syndrome — the sixth childhood illness appears only in child Transition policies and child critical illness riders

The full adult list, the age at which the rider ends and any conversion right for the child are not fully set out in public materials — confirm in the rider wording.

Strengths, weak spots and traps

Strengths: one rider for all children, including future ones; low cost relative to separate policies.

Weak spots: a small amount; it depends on the parent’s policy and is not the child’s own asset.

Traps: the rider covers childhood events — adult coverage needs the child’s own policy; childhood illnesses end at 25.
