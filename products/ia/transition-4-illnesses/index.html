一句话

重疾理赔里最常见的就那几种病。Transition 4 种疾病版只保危及生命的癌症、心脏病发作、中风和冠脉搭桥，iA 引用的行业数据显示这四种占个人重疾理赔约 85%（Munich Re 2022 调查，见 Transition 客户指南）。用更低的价格先把最大头的风险盖住，是它存在的理由。

适合谁、不适合谁

适合：

  • 刚成家、预算紧的年轻家庭：先有，再求全。iA 自己的定位就是「更实惠、投保流程更快、更多人能买到」。
  • 有房贷的人：定期（10/20/25 年）可以选保额每年递减、像房贷摊还表一样降到初始的 50% 后不再降，只有 4 种病定期才有这个选项。
  • 已经有一份 25 种病保单、想用低成本在某个阶段「加厚」核心四病保障的人——iA 指南明确建议两种版本按不同期限、保额搭配。
  • 孩子：同样含 5 种儿童病，出生即可投保。

不适合：

  • 家族里有神经系统疾病（多发性硬化、帕金森、运动神经元病）、器官衰竭等风险的人——这些只在 25 种病版本里。
  • 只想买一份重疾、而且预算允许的人：25 种病版本补的是「少见但打击大」的尾部，只买一份时尾部空白更难补。
  • 想要免体检的人：4 种病版本仍是全核保，免体检要看 CancerGuard。

优点、短板与常被忽略的坑

优点

  • 花小钱盖住大头。 四种病占理赔绝大多数，是最有性价比的入门重疾。
  • 递减保额对得上房贷。 房贷越还越少，保额同步往下走，不为已经还掉的本金付钱。
  • Prevention+ 一样不少。 早期乳腺导管原位癌、早期甲状腺癌、冠脉血管成形等，在 4 种病版本里同样能领 15%。

短板

  • 尾部风险是空白。 失明、瘫痪、多发性硬化等在这一版都不赔。iA 公开手册里的一则理赔故事正是失明理赔——那种情况 4 种病版本赔不了。
  • 递减到 50% 后不再回升。 房贷提前还清以后，剩下的保额就固定在一半；如果那时您更需要重疾保障，要另买，而届时要按年龄和健康重新核保。

常被忽略的坑

  1. 冠脉「搭桥」赔、「血管成形」只赔 15%。 客户指南的例子：同样是冠脉堵塞，做开胸搭桥符合全额定义；如果能用球囊扩张（血管成形）治疗，只走 Prevention+ 的部分赔付。医生选哪种治疗，您决定不了。
  2. 中风的定义有持续时间要求。 客户指南写明中风要求新的神经症状与体征持续至少 30 天；按这个写法，几天内就恢复的轻微发作通常够不上，具体看保单定义。
  3. 递减型只在定期上有。 选了终身或到 75 岁的，就没有递减选项。

常见问题

4 种病和 25 种病价格差多少？ 视年龄、性别、吸烟与否、保额而定，本页不列价格，可请顾问同时报两种版本对照。

递减型和平准型可以中途互换吗？ 公开资料只说保额可在出单后按当时行政规则调整，增加保额要重新提供可保证明；具体能否改结构以 iA 答复为准。

4 种病版本也有儿童病吗？ 有，同样含 5 种儿童病，确诊须在 25 岁生日前。

iA Financial Group · Critical illness insurance

iA Financial Group Transition – 4 Illnesses

Covers only the four most common critical illnesses — cancer, heart attack, stroke, coronary bypass — at a lower price; term coverage can decrease to 50% to match a mortgage.

In-depth guide · checked 2026-11-19 · 5 public sources · scenarios are hypothetical arithmetic, not quotes or promises.

In one sentence

A handful of illnesses make up most critical illness claims. Transition 4 covers life-threatening cancer, heart attack, stroke and coronary artery bypass surgery, which iA says account for about 85% of individual critical illness claims in the industry (Munich Re 2022 survey, quoted in the client guide). Covering the biggest risks first at a lower price is the point.

Who it fits — and who it does not

Fits:

  • Young families on a tight budget: get covered first, broaden later. iA describes it as more affordable, quicker to apply for and accessible to more people.
  • Mortgage holders: 10/20/25-year terms can decrease each year like an amortization schedule down to 50% of the original amount — available only on 4-illness term coverage.
  • People who already hold a 25-illness policy and want a low-cost top-up of the core four for a period; iA’s guide suggests combining versions.
  • Children: the same 5 childhood illnesses are included, insurable from birth.

Does not fit:

  • Families with risks such as multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s, motor neuron disease or organ failure — those are only in the 25-illness version.
  • Someone buying only one critical illness policy who can afford broader cover.
  • People who need no-exam coverage — this is fully underwritten; see Cancer Guard.

Strengths, weak spots and easily missed traps

Strengths: covers the bulk of claims at entry-level cost; decreasing coverage tracks a mortgage so you do not pay for principal already repaid; Prevention+ is fully included.

Weak spots: the tail is uncovered — blindness, paralysis, MS and others pay nothing (one of the claim stories in iA’s own brochure is a blindness claim, which this version would not pay); once decreased to 50%, coverage stays there, and buying more later means new underwriting at an older age.

Traps

  1. Bypass pays in full, angioplasty pays 15%. In the guide’s example the same blocked artery qualifies for the full amount if treated by open bypass surgery, but only a Prevention+ partial payment if treated by balloon angioplasty — a choice your doctor makes, not you.
  2. Stroke needs lasting symptoms. New neurological symptoms and deficits must persist at least 30 days; a mild episode that resolves within days usually does not qualify — check the policy definition.
  3. Decreasing amounts exist only on term coverage, not on to-75 or permanent coverage.

Frequently asked questions

How much cheaper is it than 25 illnesses? It depends on age, sex, smoking status and amount; ask for both quotes side by side.

Can I switch between decreasing and level later? Public materials only say the amount can be changed after issue under the rules then in force, with evidence required for increases; ask iA about structure changes.

Does the 4-illness version include childhood illnesses? Yes, the same five, diagnosed before age 25.
