一句话

Kiddy Plan 是 iA 给 2 到 5 岁孩子的一份免费重疾保障：12 个月内，家里的孩子若首次确诊列明的重疾，赔 2,000 元，每个家庭一次；到期不自动续保，也没有购买义务（iA Kiddy Plan 页）。它的价值不在那 2,000 元，而在于让父母第一次认真想一想：孩子真生了重病，家里的钱怎么撑。

适合谁、不适合谁

适合：

  • 家里有 2–5 岁孩子、还没给孩子买过任何保障的家庭：零成本先有一层。
  • 想了解重疾险是什么、但暂时不想投入保费的父母。

不适合：

  • 把它当成孩子的正式保障的人：保额 2,000、只保 12 个月、一个家庭只赔一次，远不足以覆盖父母停工的成本。
  • 孩子已经确诊过重疾的家庭：投保时已有重疾诊断的不符合条件。

优点、短板与常被忽略的坑

优点：免费；手续简单；一份覆盖家里几个符合年龄的孩子。

短板：保额很小、期限一年、全家只赔一次；病种以公开页列举为准，完整清单要看计划条款。

常被忽略的坑

  1. 「全家一次」不是「每个孩子一次」。 两个孩子先后确诊，只赔第一次。
  2. 12 个月一到就没了。 没有提醒续保这回事，想要长期保障要另外安排。
  3. 登记会带来顾问联络。 公开页写明附带顾问的免费支持；不想被推销，可以只登记、不约谈。

iA Financial Group · Free children's critical illness plan

iA Financial Group Kiddy Plan

Free 12-month critical illness cover for children aged 2–5 — $2,000 on the first critical illness diagnosis, once per family; no automatic renewal and no obligation to buy.

In-depth guide · checked 2026-11-19 · 2 public sources · scenarios are hypothetical arithmetic, not quotes or promises.

In one sentence

Kiddy Plan is iA’s free critical illness cover for children aged 2 to 5: for 12 months, if a child in the family is first diagnosed with a listed critical illness, iA pays $2,000, once per family; it does not renew automatically and carries no obligation to buy (iA Kiddy Plan page). Its value is less the $2,000 than the prompt to ask how the family would cope financially if a child fell seriously ill.

Who it fits — and who it does not

Fits: families with children aged 2–5 who have no cover yet; parents curious about critical illness insurance who do not want to pay yet.

Does not fit: anyone treating it as real protection — $2,000, 12 months, once per family; children already diagnosed with a critical illness at enrolment are not eligible.

How it works

Item Detail

Diagnosis By a specialist authorised to practise in Canada; diagnoses outside Canada only if iA’s conditions are met, which may include an independent medical exam
Limits One plan covers all eligible children; one free coverage per child for life
After 12 months No automatic renewal, no obligation to purchase
Enrol Online or 1-888-266-2224; comes with a mini first-aid kit

Strengths, weak spots and traps

Strengths: free, simple, covers all eligible children. Weak spots: tiny amount, one year, one claim per family. Traps: “once per family” is not once per child; it simply ends after 12 months; enrolment includes advisor support — you can enrol without booking a meeting.
