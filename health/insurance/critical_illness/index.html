重疾险

这是什么

确诊约定重疾（癌症、心脏病、中风等）后一次性给付一笔钱，与是否身故无关。

健康怎么影响它

重疾赔的正是癌症、心脏病、中风这几类，所以对相关病史比寿险更严：常见做法是对相关病种除外，或直接拒保；与赔付病种无关的小问题可能照常承保。

这个险种可能出现的结果

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ●除外
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

被拒或延期时还能看什么

部分公司有只答少量问题的简化重疾，保额与病种较少。

✅官方
  • Financial Consumer Agency of Canada · Health insurance （取用 2026-11-19）

Health and insurability atlas / By insurance type

Critical illness insurance

What it is

Pays a one-time lump sum on diagnosis of a covered illness such as cancer, heart attack or stroke.

How health affects it

Because it pays on the very conditions underwriters worry about, it is stricter than life insurance on related history: exclusions for the related illness or a decline are common, while unrelated minor issues may still be standard.

Outcomes possible for this type

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ●Exclusion
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

If declined or postponed

Some insurers offer simplified critical illness with fewer questions, lower amounts and fewer covered conditions.

✅ Official
  • Financial Consumer Agency of Canada · Health insurance (accessed 2026-11-19)
