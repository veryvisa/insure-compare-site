旅游与访客医疗险

这是什么

出省出国或来加探亲时的紧急医疗。

健康怎么影响它

关键是「既往症稳定条款」：出发前一段时间内病情稳定（没换药、没新症状、没新检查）才保这个病；不稳定的只是这个病不赔，不是整张保单作废。年长者常要答健康问卷分档。

这个险种可能出现的结果

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ●除外
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ●拒保

被拒或延期时还能看什么

有的产品可选更短稳定期或含既往症的方案（价更高）；访客险不少产品对既往症只保有限额度或不保。

Health and insurability atlas / By insurance type

Travel and visitor medical insurance

What it is

Emergency medical cover when travelling outside your province or visiting Canada.

How health affects it

The key is the pre-existing condition stability clause: the condition is covered only if it was stable (no medication change, new symptoms or new tests) for a period before departure. If unstable, claims for that condition are excluded, not the whole policy. Older travellers often answer a medical questionnaire that sets the rate tier.

Outcomes possible for this type

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ●Exclusion
  • ○Postponed (not typical)
  • ●Declined

If declined or postponed

Some plans offer shorter stability periods or pre-existing condition options at higher cost; many visitor plans cover pre-existing conditions only partly or not at all.

❓ To verify
  • Office of Consumer Affairs (ISED) · Travel (accessed 2026-11-19)
  • Financial Consumer Agency of Canada · Health insurance (accessed 2026-11-19)
