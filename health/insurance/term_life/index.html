健康与投保图谱 / 按险种查

定期寿险

这是什么

在约定年限内身故赔一笔钱，保家人收入与房贷。

健康怎么影响它

核保看「未来多少年内身故风险有多高」：慢病控制、癌症距今年数、心脑血管事件最关键。投保额越高、年龄越大，要的体检越多。

这个险种可能出现的结果

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

被拒或延期时还能看什么

被拒或延期时，可看简化核保（只答问卷）或保证承保（不问健康、保额小、头两年多为退保费）产品。

这个险种下的常见病

高血压

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

控制良好多为标准；读数偏高或合并其他心血管风险会加费；控制很差先延期。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

2 型糖尿病

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ●拒保

多数会加费；年纪较大、控制好、无并发症时有机会标准；合并心、脑、肾病多拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

1 型糖尿病

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ●拒保

通常加费，控制越好、无并发症加得越少；有严重并发症多拒保。个别公司对极好控制可标准。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

高胆固醇

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ○拒保（通常不出现）

三份公开指南都没有单列；一般规律是单独的、控制好的高胆固醇多为标准，主要作为心血管风险的加分项一起看。

依据：一般规律，待核

乳腺癌史

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

治疗中与结束后一段时间多为延期；之后按分期加费，年数越久加得越少；原位癌几年后可能标准。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

甲状腺癌史

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

早期、小的乳头状癌在治疗结束一段时间后常可标准；其他类型或较晚分期等待更久或加费。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

前列腺癌史

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

确诊后短期内多延期；早期、治疗多年且 PSA 稳定可标准；其余按年数加费递减。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

冠心病（含支架、搭桥）

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

手术或事件后一段时间多延期；之后通常加费；发病很年轻或多处病变多拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

中风与短暂性脑缺血（TIA）

  • ○标准（通常不出现）
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

发作后一段时间延期或拒保；之后按后遗症与年龄加费；多次发作或严重后遗症多拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

房颤

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ●延期
  • ●拒保

偶发、心脏结构正常可标准；持续性或合并心脏病加费；新近发现或有中风史可能延期或拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

哮喘

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ●拒保

轻中度、控制好多为标准；频繁发作、住院或吸烟加费；极重度可能拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

慢阻肺（COPD）

  • ●标准
  • ●加费
  • ○除外（通常不出现）
  • ○延期（通常不出现）
  • ●拒保

轻度、已戒烟可能标准或小幅加费；中重度加费；重度或仍吸烟多拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

睡眠呼吸暂停

  • ●拒保

轻度或坚持治疗多为标准；中重度未治疗加费；合并严重心肺问题可能拒保。

依据：两份以上公开核保指南一致

抑郁与焦虑

  • ●标准

  • ○延期（通常不出现）

轻度、稳定多为标准；较重或反复加费；近期住院、自杀行为或正在停工多拒保或延后再议。

Health and insurability atlas / By insurance type

Term life insurance

What it is

Pays a lump sum if you die within the term; protects family income and the mortgage.

How health affects it

Underwriting asks how likely death is during the term: control of chronic disease, years since cancer treatment and heart or brain events matter most. Larger amounts and older ages trigger more tests.

Outcomes possible for this type

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

If declined or postponed

If declined or postponed, simplified-issue (questions only) or guaranteed-issue (no health questions, small amounts, usually a return of premiums in the first two years) products may be options.

Common conditions for this type

High blood pressure (hypertension)

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Well controlled is usually standard; higher readings or other cardiac risks lead to a rating; very poor control is postponed.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

Type 2 diabetes

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ○Postponed (not typical)
  • ●Declined

Most are rated; older applicants with good control and no complications may get standard; with heart, stroke or kidney disease, decline is likely.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

Type 1 diabetes

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ○Postponed (not typical)
  • ●Declined

Usually rated; better control without complications means a lower rating; serious complications usually lead to decline. A few insurers may offer standard for excellent control.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

High cholesterol

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ○Postponed (not typical)
  • ○Declined (not typical)

None of the three public guides lists it separately; generally, controlled high cholesterol alone is standard and is weighed together with other cardiac risks.

Basis: General pattern, to be verified

History of breast cancer

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Usually postponed during and for a while after treatment; then rated by stage, with the rating falling as years pass; in-situ cases may become standard after some years.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

History of thyroid cancer

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Small early papillary cancers can often be standard some time after treatment; other types or later stages wait longer or are rated.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

History of prostate cancer

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Usually postponed shortly after diagnosis; early-stage cases treated years ago with stable PSA may be standard; others are rated, falling with time.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

Coronary artery disease (incl. stent or bypass)

  • ●Standard
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Usually postponed for a while after a procedure or event, then rated; very young onset or extensive disease is often declined.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

Stroke and TIA (mini-stroke)

  • ○Standard (not typical)
  • ●Rated (extra premium)
  • ○Exclusion (not typical)
  • ●Postponed
  • ●Declined

Postponed or declined for a period after the event, then rated by residual effects and age; repeated events or major deficits usually decline.

Basis: Two or more public underwriting guides agree

Atrial fibrillation

  • ○Exclusion (not typical)

  • ●Declined

Occasional episodes with a normal heart can be standard; persistent AF or heart disease is rated; newly found or with prior stroke may be postponed or declined.

  • ○Exclusion (not typical)

  • ●Declined

Mild to moderate controlled asthma is usually standard; frequent attacks, hospital stays or smoking lead to ratings; very severe cases may be declined.

Mild cases who have quit may be standard or lightly rated; moderate to severe are rated; severe or still smoking usually decline.

Mild or well-treated cases are usually standard; untreated moderate to severe cases are rated; with serious heart or lung problems, decline is possible.

  • ○Postponed (not typical)
  • ●Declined

Mild, stable cases are usually standard; more severe or recurrent ones are rated; recent hospital stays, suicide attempts or current time off work usually decline or are reconsidered later.

  • ○Declined (not typical)

Obesity and BMI bands
