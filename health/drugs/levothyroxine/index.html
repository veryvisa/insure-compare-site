Levothyroxine

What it is

Replaces thyroid hormone for an underactive thyroid or after thyroid removal.

✅ Official

How underwriters read this medication

Plain hypothyroidism on a stable dose is usually standard; underwriters check whether it follows surgery for thyroid cancer.
