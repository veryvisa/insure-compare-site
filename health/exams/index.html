体检与健康证明

保额越高、年龄越大，保险公司要的健康证明越多。这叫「年龄与保额要求」，每家公司有自己的表，下面是共同规律，不是任何一家的具体门槛。

✅官方
  • Financial Consumer Agency of Canada · Getting an insurance policy （取用 2026-11-19）
  • BMO Insurance · Field Underwriting Manual – Life Insurance (984E) （取用 2026-11-19）

共同规律

  1. 年轻、保额小：多半只答问卷，可能加一次电话访谈。
  2. 保额或年龄上一个台阶：加辅助体检与血尿检。
  3. 更高保额或较大年龄：可能加心电图、更多血检与财务证明。
  4. 任何年龄只要有相关病史：可能调主治医生报告或要专病问卷。

各项是什么

健康问卷
投保书上的健康问题，必须如实回答；答错可能导致日后拒赔或保单失效。
电话访谈
核保员或护士电话复核健康与生活习惯。
辅助体检（paramedical）
护士上门或在体检点量身高体重、血压、脉搏，并问病史。
血液与尿检
看血糖（A1C）、血脂、肝肾功能、尼古丁等。
心电图
年龄较大或保额较高时常要；有心脏病史时会要更多心脏检查。
主治医生报告（APS）
保险公司经你授权向医生调病历；有病史时最常见，也是等待时间最长的一环。
专病问卷
如呼吸、精神健康、糖尿病问卷，让核保员不必等病历就能初判。
财务证明
保额高时要证明保额与收入、资产相称。

少等几周的做法

  • 先问顾问「这个病大概会怎么判」——很多公司接受先不正式投保的预审（trial / pre-assessment）。
  • 备好确诊日期、用药名称与剂量、最近检查结果，可以明显缩短等待。

Medical exams and evidence

The larger the amount and the older the applicant, the more evidence of health an insurer asks for. These 'age and amount requirements' differ by insurer; below are the common patterns, not any company's actual thresholds.

✅ Official
  • Financial Consumer Agency of Canada · Getting an insurance policy (accessed 2026-11-19)
  • BMO Insurance · Field Underwriting Manual – Life Insurance (984E) (accessed 2026-11-19)

Common patterns

  1. Young applicants, smaller amounts: often questions only, perhaps a tele-interview.
  2. Larger amounts or older ages: paramedical plus blood and urine tests.
  3. Larger still, or older still: ECG, more blood work and financial evidence may be added.
  4. At any age, a relevant health history can bring an APS or a condition questionnaire.

What each item is

Health questionnaire
Health questions on the application; answer truthfully — inaccurate answers can void the policy or lead to a denied claim.
Tele-interview
A nurse or underwriter reviews health and lifestyle by phone.
Paramedical exam
A nurse measures height, weight, blood pressure and pulse at home or a clinic and records history.
Blood and urine tests
Checks blood sugar (A1C), cholesterol, liver and kidney function, nicotine and more.
ECG
Common at older ages or larger amounts; more cardiac tests if there is heart history.
Attending physician statement (APS)
With your consent the insurer requests records from your doctor; most common with a health history and often the slowest step.
Condition-specific questionnaire
Such as respiratory, mental health or diabetes questionnaires, helping a tentative decision before records arrive.
Financial evidence
For large amounts, proof that the amount fits income and assets.

Ways to save weeks

  • Ask your advisor for a likely decision first — many insurers accept an informal trial or pre-assessment.
  • Having diagnosis dates, medication names and doses and recent results ready shortens the wait.
