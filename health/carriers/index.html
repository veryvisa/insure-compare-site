各保险公司怎么问、怎么核：健康问卷与核保要求对比

同一个客户去哪家更顺？这里把 12 家寿险公司在 8 个方面的公开说法摆在一起。只用各公司公开、免登录的核保指南、投保说明和常见问题；每一格带来源与取用日期，查不到的写「未公开」，不猜。

顾问怎么用这张表

  • 想少做体检、保额又大：iA、BMO、RBC、Empire Life、Foresters、Assumption 都公开了免体检额度，年龄段与上限各不相同。BMO 写明加速核保不适用重疾险，也不适用来加拿大不满 12 个月的新移民；其他公司公开材料没写这类限制，但没写不等于没有，要向顾问确认现行规定。
  • 用大麻或电子烟的客户：BMO 写明吸电子烟（含无尼古丁）即算吸烟者，单纯大麻可按非吸烟；Equitable 写明吸入或食用大麻可按非吸烟；Beneva 写明大麻与烟草同用按吸烟；Foresters 的重疾计划对大麻有每周次数线。同一个人在不同公司的烟草分档可能不同。
  • 家族史敏感的客户（父母较早患心脏病或癌症）：Equitable 的简易重疾写明 2 位以上直系亲属 60 岁前患心脏病、中风、癌症等即不合格；Beneva 的优选档按 60 岁前看；BMO 没有统一年龄线，看亲属人数与发病年龄。年龄线与算法不同，同一份家族史会得到不同的答案。
  • 只想要快速、不体检的简易产品：Equitable 的简易签发重疾约 7 道是否题、Foresters 的重疾简化计划 6 至 8 道资格题，Assumption 按四个等级用同一份问卷定费率；其他公司只说「几道健康问题」，题数未公开，要先问顾问。
  • 给孩子买重疾：Desjardins 的儿童重疾覆盖 31 天至 25 岁、可加 3 种儿童疾病；iA Kiddy 是 2 到 5 岁的免费一年计划；Foresters 有儿童重疾附加险但没公开投保年龄。其余公司公开材料里没有查到儿童重疾的投保年龄与健康问题。

    How an advisor can use this

    • Few exams, large amount: iA, BMO, RBC, Empire Life, Foresters and Assumption publish no-exam limits, with different age bands and caps. BMO states its accelerated underwriting excludes critical illness and people in Canada under 12 months; the others do not state such limits in public material, which does not mean there are none, so confirm current rules.
    • Cannabis or vaping clients: BMO counts any vaping (even nicotine-free) as smoking while cannabis alone can rate non-smoker; Equitable says inhaled or edible cannabis can rate non-smoker; Beneva rates cannabis used with tobacco as smoking; Foresters' CI plan sets a weekly cannabis limit. The same person can land in different classes.
    • Strong family history: Equitable's simplified CI declines two or more close relatives with heart disease, stroke or cancer before 60; Beneva's preferred class also looks before 60; BMO has no single age line and weighs the number of relatives and age of onset. The same history can read differently.
    • Fast, no-exam simplified products: Equitable's simplified CI has about 7 yes/no questions, Foresters' simplified CI 6 to 8 eligibility questions, and Assumption prices four tiers from one questionnaire; other insurers only say a few health questions, so ask your advisor first.
    • Critical illness for a child: Desjardins covers 31 days to age 25 with three child illnesses addable; iA Kiddy is a free first year for ages 2 to 5; Foresters offers a juvenile CI rider without published ages. The other insurers' public material did not show child CI ages or health questions.
